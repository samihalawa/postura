# Contributing to Postura

Build with `./build.sh` on a Mac with Xcode command-line tools. Open `build/Postura.app` and allow camera
access. The app sets up its local MediaPipe environment on first launch.

For posture or cover changes, check sustained bad posture, corrected posture and an empty camera frame.
For camera changes, check the Dock preview and feedback capture. Include your macOS version, hardware,
Postura version and steps to reproduce. Keep camera images, numerical calibration data and personal
log contents out of issues and pull requests.
