A POSTURE COACH FOR MAC

Catch the slouch.
Sit a little taller.

Postura notices when you slouch and gradually covers your screen. Sit up and the cover eases away. The camera and posture analysis stay on your Mac.

Download for Mac ↓ See how it works

macOS 14+ · Apple silicon and Intel · Free and open source

HOW IT WORKS

A pause you can feel.

It waits for a sustained slouch.

A brief movement won’t start the cover. After five seconds of clearly bad posture, a soft glow begins to close in.

Sit up to clear the screen.

The cover recedes once you correct your posture. If you step out of frame, the screen clears immediately.

Teach it what feels right.

Use the Dock window to label your good and bad posture live. Postura uses those examples to personalise its judgments.

CHALLENGES

Make a commitment.

Choose when a one-time lock starts, how long it lasts, and what happens if it overlaps your daily hours. Set those hours separately, with an optional 7-, 14-, or 30-day challenge. If you open Postura after the daily start, it skips that occurrence.

Find it in Settings → Challenges.

INSTALL

Start on your Mac.

Open Postura and allow camera access. The first launch sets up the local posture model. The Dock opens the live camera, feedback and settings.

QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Are any camera frames saved or sent?

Postura processes frames on your Mac. It keeps numerical posture examples locally when you label an example.

What happens during video calls?

When Postura detects a call, it limits the reminder to a faint edge glow.

Can Postura remind me to wear my glasses?

Yes. Turn on Require glasses in Settings and record a live sample with and without your glasses.

Can I pause a challenge?

Posture and Glasses each have three two-minute resets per day. Healthy Lockout has one reset per week that grants two hours of access before the lock resumes.
